Pistach

A personal iPhone planner: calendar, reminders, to-dos and countdowns in one place, each tagged Pro or Perso, with a short morning recap of the day.

What it does

  • Today: a morning recap, today's to-dos split Pro / Perso, the day's calendar and what's coming up.
  • Calendar: two-way sync with the owner's Google Calendar accounts.
  • Recap: reads unread or starred Gmail headers and recently modified Google Drive file names to flag what needs attention. It never sends email and never reads file contents.
  • Countdowns and home-screen widgets.

Who it's for

Pistach is a personal project built and used by Ben Souchet for his own accounts. It is not distributed on the App Store and is not offered to the public.
